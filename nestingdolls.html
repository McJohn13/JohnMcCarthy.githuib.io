Nest Doll Project

Descrition: This was a small project that I did, where I made something of my choosing about the holiday season and winter.
Programs: Autodesk Maya, Adobe Substance Painter

Modeling

For the modeling I started out with a basic sphere for the head and then extruded the bottom to get a basic shape of the body. I then added horizontal edges along the body and edited them to get a more rounded body. The hat is a cylinder that I extruded inward to get the space for the head, then I added an edge and extruded to create the brim.

Texturing

All the textures were made by me. My thought process behind the textures was to make it look like painted wood. This required fill layers and paint layers to get that look.

Reflection

I learned a lot from this small project. I learned how to make my own textures in Substance Painter. I also learned how important the lighting is the scene.
