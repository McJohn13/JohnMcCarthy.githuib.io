Product Ad Project

Descrition: For this project, I had to find a transparent bottle and recreate it in Maya. The texturing had to be done in Photoshop. The objective for this was to create a visually striking ad that communicates what the brand wants. This is meant to be simulating "real world" experience doing work for a company or brand.
Programs: Autodesk Maya, Adobe Photoshop

Modeling

I started the modeling process by finding a reference image of a transparent bottle and using it as a template to create the 3D model in Maya. After that i was able to create the label and liquid inside the bottle by copying the bottle and deleting parts of the bottle to get the desired shape.

Texturing

Both of the textures were created in Adobe Photoshop. The cap only uses a cork texture to give it the cork look. The label uses a gradietnt for the base. It also uses two ovals, one black and one of the base gradient but flipped to give it that floating disk look.

AOV's

I used AOV's to separate the different elements of the render to have more control over the final image. I used a diffuse, specular, and shadow AOV to be able to adjust the lighting and shadows in post processing.

Reflection

This project helped reinforce and expand my knowledge of 3D modeling and texturing techniques.
